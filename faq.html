Frequently Asked Questions

Is VidGrab Pro free to use?
Yes, this website is completely free, with no accounts or subscriptions. Keep in mind that the third-party downloader sites it routes you to are supported by their own advertising.
What video qualities are available?
VidGrab Pro does not process videos itself - the downloader service that opens in a new tab determines which qualities and formats are offered. Most services provide a range from SD up to Full HD when the source allows it.
Is it legal to download videos?
VidGrab Pro is intended for personal use. Please respect copyright laws and only download videos you own or have permission to use. Be aware that downloading may also violate the terms of service of some platforms.
Do you support mobile devices?
Yes, this website is fully responsive and works on smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers.
Why is my download not working?
The actual download happens on the third-party service that opens in a new tab. Those services change frequently and may have temporary issues, ads, or pop-ups. Try again, use a different browser, or report persistent problems via our contact page.
Can I download entire playlists?
No. VidGrab Pro works with individual video links only.
How long does it take to download a video?
That depends on the destination service, your internet connection, and the size of the video. VidGrab Pro itself adds no delay - it simply opens the right page for your link.
Do you store any of my data?
This website stores nothing and has no backend database. However, note that when you submit a URL, it is passed along to the third-party downloader site that opens - their own privacy policy applies there.
Do I need to create an account?
No. There are no accounts on VidGrab Pro at all - just paste a link and go.
What formats are supported?
Formats are determined by the destination downloader service. MP4 is the most commonly offered format across services.
