How It Works

Finding a downloader for your video is simple and fast. Follow these four easy steps:

Step 1: Copy Video URL

Go to your favorite video platform (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, etc.) and copy the video URL from your browser's address bar.

Step 2: Paste URL

Return to VidGrab Pro and paste the copied URL into the input field on our homepage. You can use the paste button or simply press Ctrl+V.

Step 3: Platform Detection

VidGrab Pro detects which platform your link belongs to (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Twitter/X and more).

Step 4: Get Rerouted

A matching third-party downloader website opens in a new tab with your link pre-filled. Follow that site's instructions to save the video. Available quality options depend on the service.
